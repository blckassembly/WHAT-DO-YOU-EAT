# WHAT DO YOU EAT? Creator Sponsorship & Food Commerce Platform

A creator sponsorship and food commerce platform connecting live-streamed Twitch food moments directly to viewers' de facto food apps (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Wonder App, Wingstop App, Ralph's Coffee App, Shake Shack App) with a 3-minute autonomous simulation workflow and a dedicated streamer Discord deck.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **Autonomous 3-Minute Simulation (0:00 - 03:00)**: Users can click **"Run 3-Minute Simulation"** and watch the full end-to-end Twitch broadcast workflow unfold automatically without manual button clicks or barcode physics:
>   - *Phase 1 (0:00 - 0:30)*: Streamer goes live, viewers flood in (ramping from 85K $\to$ 1.45M), lobby hype chat. Overlay is hidden.
>   - *Phase 2 (0:30 - 1:15)*: Gameplay in flow, streamer talks smack, mentions being starving as food pulls up (over 2.2M viewers).
>   - *Phase 3 (1:15 - 1:50)*: Sponsorship overlay drops smoothly on stream; bot announces promo in chat (`!food` and code `KAIWONDER` / `VOODOODROP`).
>   - *Phase 4 (1:50 - 2:25)*: Mobile phone camera scan automatically slides into view, locks onto the stream QR code, and launches the viewer's de facto app (Wonder App, Uber Eats).
>   - *Phase 5 (2:25 - 3:00)*: Real-time commerce surge with streamer Discord bot webhook pinging attributed orders and kickback payouts.
> - **Wonder Multi-Restaurant & Wingstop Voodoo Loaded Fries Support**: Added Wonder (Di Fara Pizza + Wingstop Voodoo Fries + Tejas BBQ delivered together in 1 courier bag) and Wingstop with Voodoo Cajun Cheese Fries.
> - **Streamer Discord User Interface**: Provided a dedicated Discord console (`#sponsorship-bot`) showing what the streamer sees: real-time conversion webhooks, viewer preferred app distributions, and Stripe Connect kickbacks.
> - **Signature Bottle Green Palette (`#10382E` / `#0C2B23` / `#164D3F`)**: Retained the deep Ralph's Coffee bottle green aesthetic throughout.
